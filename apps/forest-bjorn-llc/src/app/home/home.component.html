Forest Bjorn is a boutique consultancy for small teams whose operations have outgrown scattered tools, manual follow-up, and disconnected data. We bring senior engineering judgment to the unglamorous systems that make the business easier to run.

That can look like an AI-assisted intake flow, a private knowledge assistant, a customer portal, a reporting dashboard, or a custom internal tool. The point is the same: connect the work, reduce the drag, and make the system reliable enough to trust.
